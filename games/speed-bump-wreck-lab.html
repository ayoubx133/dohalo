FAQ

Questions and answers

Is Speed Bump Wreck Lab free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play. It shows ads served by Google AdMob.

Which devices can I play on?

Speed Bump Wreck Lab is made for iPhone and iPad.

When can I download it?

The game has been submitted to the App Store and is coming soon. Once it is live, the View on the App Store button on this page takes you straight to it.

How do I play Trap Run?

Swipe left or right to move between the three lanes and hold the gas to speed up. Smash through road blocks and explosive barrels, and dodge the hammers, wrecking balls, crushers and saw blades that guard the road.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no account and no sign up. Your progress is saved on your device. If you delete the game, your progress is deleted too.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open the game and go to Settings then Privacy options to review or change your ad consent. To control tracking across apps, use your device Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

I cannot hear any music or sound.

Check that your iPhone or iPad is not in Silent mode and that the volume is turned up. If you use Bluetooth headphones, make sure they are connected.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email support@dohalo.com with your device model, iOS version and a short description of what happened. A screenshot or screen recording helps a lot.
