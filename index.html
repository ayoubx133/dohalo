The studio

Small studio. Big crash button.

Dohalo Games is the trading name of Doha Harf, an independent developer making 3D action games for iPhone and iPad.

We make games you can enjoy in short bursts: simple touch controls, quick levels and a satisfying wreck at the end of every run. Our first title, Speed Bump Wreck Lab, is on its way to the App Store, and nine more games are already in the workshop.

How we make games

Four rules for every Dohalo game

Whatever the theme, these ideas come first.

1

Physics first

Cars bend, crates scatter and walls give way. The fun is built on real 3D physics, so no two runs play out the same.

2

Quick to learn

Simple touch controls and short levels you can finish in a minute, with enough depth to keep you coming back.

3

Plenty to earn

Levels, worlds, vehicles, upgrades and paints. There is always something new to unlock.

4

Fair to players

No account and no sign up. Progress stays on your device, and your ad privacy choices are always in Settings.
