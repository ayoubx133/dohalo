?Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress, unlocked cars, coins and settings are saved on your device. There are no accounts and no cloud saves, so if you delete the game, your progress is deleted too and we cannot get it back. Keep the game installed to keep your levels and cars.

How do I restore a purchase?

If a game offers an in-app purchase, such as removing ads, open the game and go to Settings then Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Restoring is free. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page, because Apple processes all payments.

How do I change my ad consent or privacy options?

Open the game and go to Settings then Privacy options to review or change your ad consent (shown to players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland). To control tracking across apps, go to your device Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

There is no sound. What can I do?
  • Make sure your iPhone or iPad is not in Silent mode. On many iPhones this is the Ring/Silent switch on the side; on newer models it is set with the Action button or in Control Center.
  • Turn up the volume with the side buttons while the game is open.
  • If you use Bluetooth headphones or a speaker, check that they are connected, or turn Bluetooth off to use the device speaker.
  • Close the game fully and open it again.
The game feels slow or stutters.
  • Close other apps running in the background.
  • Turn off Low Power Mode, which can reduce performance.
  • Restart your device and make sure iOS and the game are up to date.
  • Keep some free storage on your device.
  • If your device is hot, let it cool down for a few minutes.
How do I report a bug?

Email support@dohalo.com and include:

  • the game name;
  • your device model (for example iPhone 15 or iPad Air);
  • your iOS or iPadOS version (Settings > General > About);
  • what you were doing when the problem happened;
  • a screenshot or screen recording, if you can.
Is the game free?

Yes. Our released games are free to download and play, and are supported by ads served by Google AdMob.

!Request data deletion

We keep very little data, and most of it is on your own device. Here is how to delete each kind:

Game progress and settings

Stored only on your device. Delete the game to delete it permanently.

Emails you sent us

Email support@dohalo.com with the subject "Data deletion request" from the same address. We delete them and confirm within one month.

Ad data

Refuse or withdraw consent in Settings > Privacy options, and turn off tracking in your device Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Ad data held by Google is managed by Google.

Anything else

Not sure what we hold? Email us and we will tell you, and delete it on request. See our Privacy Policy for all your rights.
