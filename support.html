Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Remove Ads purchase?

Open the game and go to Settings > Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it. The purchase is free to restore on all of your devices.

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress is saved on your device. There are no accounts. If you delete the game, your progress is deleted too, so keep the game installed if you want to keep your levels and cars.

How do I change my ads and privacy choices?

Open the game and go to Settings > Privacy Options to review or change your ad consent. To control tracking across apps, go to your device Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

I was charged but ads are still showing.

First try Settings > Restore Purchases. If ads still appear, email us with the game name and the date of purchase. For refunds, please use Apple's Report a Problem page, since Apple processes all payments.

The game is slow or crashes on my device.

Restart the device and make sure you are on the latest version of the game and iOS. If it still happens, email us your device model and what you were doing when it crashed.
