Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play any game published by Dohalo Games (each a "Game") on the Apple App Store. Dohalo Games is the trading name of Doha Harf, an individual developer ("we", "us", "our"). This one policy covers all of our Games and this website.

  • We do not collect any personal data ourselves, and we never sell data.
  • Our free Games show ads served by Google AdMob.
  • Your device advertising identifier (IDFA) is only used if you allow it through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Players in the EEA and UK are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown.
  • An optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • No accounts, no sign up. Game progress stays on your device.

1. Who is responsible

The controller for the purposes of this policy is Doha Harf, an individual developer trading as Dohalo Games. You can reach us at support@dohalo.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal information inside our Games. We do not run our own analytics or tracking, and we do not operate game servers that receive information about you. The only time we receive personal information is when you choose to email us (see section 8).

3. Game progress stored on your device

Your progress, such as finished levels, unlocked cars, scores and settings, is saved only on your device in the Game's private storage. We cannot see it. Deleting the Game deletes this data. There are no accounts and no sign in.

4. Advertising with Google AdMob

Our free Games show ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the Game may collect and process information such as:

  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language and approximate settings);
  • an IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (such as country or city);
  • information about ad interactions, such as ads shown and ads tapped;
  • the device advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you give permission as described below.

This information is collected and used by Google under its own terms and privacy policy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. App Tracking Transparency (IDFA)

On iPhone and iPad, a Game may show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asking whether it may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you tap "Allow", the advertising identifier (IDFA) may be shared with Google AdMob to show more relevant ads. If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not accessed and you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant. You can change this at any time in your device under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

6. Consent for players in the EEA and UK

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the Game shows a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised ads can be shown. You can accept, refuse or manage your choices. You can review or change your choice at any time from the Game's Settings > Privacy Options. If you do not consent, you may still see non personalised or limited ads.

7. In-app purchase: Remove Ads

Our Games may offer an optional in-app purchase called "Remove Ads". Purchases are made and processed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. We do not receive or store your name, card number or any other payment information. Apple only tells the Game that the purchase was completed so it can turn ads off. You can restore a previous purchase from the Game's Settings > Restore Purchases. Apple's handling of your payment is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

8. Emails you send us

If you contact us at support@dohalo.com, we use your email address and message only to answer you. We do not add you to any mailing list, and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

9. We do not sell data

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We do not share information with anyone, except that ad related data is processed by Google AdMob and purchase data is processed by Apple, as described above.

10. Children

Our Games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads in our Games are limited to family friendly content ratings. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Permissions

Our Games do not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. The only system prompt you may see is Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt described in section 5.

12. Your choices and rights

You can limit ad tracking with the App Tracking Transparency setting, change your ad consent from Settings > Privacy Options in the Game, or stop all data processing by the Game by uninstalling it. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about players, most requests are best handled directly with Google or Apple, but we are happy to help: just email us.

13. Data security and retention

We keep only the emails you send us, and only for as long as needed to answer you. Game progress remains on your device until you delete the Game.

14. This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These services receive the standard technical data that any web server receives, such as your IP address and browser type.

15. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's update notes.
