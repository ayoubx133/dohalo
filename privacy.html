Privacy Policy

How our games and this website handle your information, in plain English.

Effective date: 16 November 2026

The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email or any account. Game progress stays on your device.
  • Our free Games show ads from Google AdMob. Your advertising identifier (IDFA) is only used if you allow it through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland see Google's consent message and can change their choice any time in Settings > Privacy options.
  • Any in-app purchase is processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell personal information. This website has no cookies and no analytics.

1Who we are

Dohalo Games is the trading name of Doha Harf, an individual developer. Doha Harf is the seller of our games on the Apple App Store and is the controller responsible for personal information described in this policy ("we", "us", "our").

2What this policy covers

This policy applies to every game we publish on the App Store for iPhone and iPad (each a "Game"), including Speed Bump Wreck Lab, and to this website, dohalo.com. Some Games may not include every feature described here, for example an in-app purchase.

3Information we do not collect

We built our Games to need as little data as possible. We do not:

  • ask you to create an account or sign in;
  • ask for your name, email address, phone number, postal address or date of birth inside a Game;
  • access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location;
  • run our own analytics, tracking or game servers that receive information about you;
  • sell your personal information.

The only personal information we receive directly is what you send us when you email us (see section 10). Advertising in our free Games is handled by Google AdMob, as explained below.

4Information stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (for example finished levels, unlocked cars, upgrades, coins, settings and your ad privacy choices) are saved only on your device, inside the Game's private storage. This information is not sent to us and we cannot see it.

Deleting the Game deletes this information. Because there are no accounts, we cannot restore progress after a Game has been deleted.

5Advertising with Google AdMob

Our free Games show ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC and its affiliates ("Google"). To show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud, the AdMob software inside the Game may collect and process:

  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size and app version;
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location, such as your country or city;
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether they were tapped, and when;
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt (see section 6);

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

6App Tracking Transparency and the IDFA

On iPhone and iPad, a Game may show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asking whether it may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites.

  • If you tap Allow, the advertising identifier (IDFA) may be shared with Google AdMob to show more relevant ads and measure them.
  • If you tap Ask App Not to Track, the Game does not access the IDFA. You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

You can change your choice at any time in your device Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

7Personalised and non personalised ads

Personalised ads are chosen using information about you, such as your advertising identifier and past ad interactions. We only allow personalised ads where you have given the permissions required in your region (App Tracking Transparency and, where it applies, Google's consent message).

Non personalised ads are chosen using contextual information, such as the app you are using and your general location (for example your country), and not your past behaviour. Google may still use cookies or identifiers for non personalised ads to limit ad frequency, report on ads and fight fraud and abuse.

8Consent for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game shows a consent message provided by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) the first time you play, before personalised ads can be shown. You can accept, refuse or manage your choices for each purpose and ad partner.

You can review or change your choice at any time: open the Game, go to Settings and tap Privacy options. If you refuse consent, you may still see non personalised or limited ads.

9In-app purchases

Some Games may offer optional in-app purchases, such as removing ads. If they do, purchases are made and processed entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see or store your name, card number or any other payment details. Apple only tells the Game that a purchase was completed so it can unlock the item. Apple's handling of your payment is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

10When you email us

If you contact us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you choose to include, such as your device model, iOS version or screenshots. We use this only to answer you and to fix the problems you report. We do not add you to a mailing list and we do not share your message with anyone else.

11Children

Our Games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads in our Games are served with settings appropriate for a general audience.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information, please email support@dohalo.com and we will delete it promptly.

12Legal bases (GDPR and UK GDPR)

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Consent: for personalised ads and for accessing or storing information on your device for that purpose. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings > Privacy options.
  • Legitimate interests: for showing non personalised ads that fund our free Games, for ad measurement, and for security and fraud prevention. You can object to this processing (see section 16).
  • Legitimate interests: for answering your emails and keeping a record of support requests.
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep or disclose information to comply with the law.

13International transfers

Google may process ad data on servers in the United States and other countries outside your own. Where personal information from the EEA, UK or Switzerland is transferred to countries without an adequacy decision, Google relies on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK and Swiss equivalents) or its certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss extensions. Emails you send us are handled through our email provider, which may also process them outside your country under similar safeguards.

14How long information is kept

  • Game progress and settings stay on your device until you delete the Game.
  • Emails are kept only as long as needed to answer your request and follow up on it, and are then deleted.
  • Ad data collected by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies.

15Security

We keep the information we hold to a minimum, which is the best protection there is. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what we hold.

16Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests, or restrict it;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that happened before;
  • data portability, where it applies;
  • complain to a supervisory authority, such as your local data protection authority in the EEA (list of authorities) or the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK (ico.org.uk).

To use any of these rights, email support@dohalo.com. We will reply within one month. Because we do not hold player data ourselves, the quickest ways to control ad data are the in-game Privacy options, the iOS Tracking setting and Google's own tools. You can also see our data deletion instructions.

17California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, gives you extra rights.

  • Categories we deal with. Through Google AdMob: identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, where allowed, and IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (ad interactions) and approximate location derived from IP address. Through email: the contact details and content you send us.
  • No sale for money. We do not sell personal information for money.
  • Sharing for cross context behavioural advertising. Information may be "shared" with Google AdMob for cross context behavioural advertising only when you allow tracking through App Tracking Transparency. You can opt out at any time by choosing Ask App Not to Track or turning tracking off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and by using Privacy options in the Game where it is shown.
  • Sensitive information. We do not collect or use sensitive personal information.
  • Minors. We do not knowingly sell or share personal information of consumers under 16.
  • Your rights. You can ask to know, delete or correct personal information, and to opt out of sharing. You can use an authorised agent. We will not treat you differently for using your rights. Email support@dohalo.com.

Residents of other US states with similar privacy laws can make the same requests in the same way.

18This website

dohalo.com uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. It is a static site:

  • It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub receives technical data such as your IP address and browser type when you visit, and may log it for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • It loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser requests the font files from Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser information. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

19Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our Games or the law change. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the Game's update notes.
